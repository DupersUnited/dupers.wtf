## 1. What the mod is

DupersUnited is a client-side Fabric mod for debugging Minecraft plugins
and servers. It does not give you dupes; it helps you find and reproduce
them. It also includes quality-of-life and rendering tools,
packet utilities for testing, server warnings, proxy helpers, and
cosmetic capes.

As a community we oppose gambling mechanics on Minecraft servers
(pay-to-win servers built around gambling). This mod is our tool against
them: use it to detect, avoid, document, and report such servers.
